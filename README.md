# LaunchPad AI

 Your first job, mapped out.**

LaunchPad AI is an AI-powered career readiness platform designed specifically for students, recent graduates, career changers, and entry-level candidates.

The platform helps candidates understand their career direction, identify skill gaps, improve their job applications, prepare for interviews, build relevant projects, and track their progress toward becoming job-ready.

## Project Status

🚧 Initial setup — product development will begin in the upcoming capstone assignments.

## Problem

Entry-level candidates often struggle to understand which roles they are suitable for, what skills they are missing, how to improve their applications, how to prepare for interviews, and what they should do next.

Existing platforms often solve only one part of this journey.

LaunchPad AI aims to connect these stages into one guided career-readiness experience.

## Development Approach

LaunchPad AI will be developed incrementally throughout the Frontend AI Engineering track.

AI coding assistants will be used for development support, documentation, debugging, testing, and code review.

All AI-generated changes will be reviewed by the developer before being accepted.
