# LaunchPad AI

**Your first job, mapped out.**

LaunchPad AI is an AI-powered career‑readiness platform designed for students, recent graduates, career changers, and entry‑level candidates. It guides users through the complete journey from understanding their current skills to preparing for interviews and tracking job applications.

The platform’s core idea is simple:

> Understand your strengths. Identify your gaps. Build evidence. Apply strategically. Prepare effectively. Get hired.

---

## 🚀 Product Vision

LaunchPad AI aims to give entry‑level candidates a clear, structured path from  
**“I don’t know where I fit”** → **“I know what to improve, what roles to target, and what to do next.”**

The platform will help users:

1. Understand their current skills and experience  
2. Explore suitable career paths  
3. Analyse job descriptions  
4. Assess job readiness  
5. Identify missing or underdeveloped skills  
6. Build a personalised learning roadmap  
7. Improve their CV and applications  
8. Prepare for interviews  
9. Build relevant portfolio projects  
10. Track applications and outcomes  
11. Learn from rejections and improve  
12. Progress toward employment  

LaunchPad AI is not a job board or a CV generator — it is a **guided career‑readiness system**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Entry‑level candidates often struggle with:

- Which roles match their skills  
- What skills they are missing  
- Whether their CV is relevant  
- What to learn next  
- How to prepare for interviews  
- Which projects to build  
- Whether they are improving  

Existing tools solve only one part of the journey.  
LaunchPad AI connects all parts into one **continuous, guided experience**.

---

## 🧩 Core Concept — Job Readiness

The central idea of the platform is **Job Readiness**.

LaunchPad AI helps candidates understand and improve areas such as:

- Skills  
- Projects  
- CV quality  
- Interview readiness  
- Role suitability  
- Career direction  
- Relevant experience  

Readiness scores will be **explanatory**, not predictive.  
They will motivate improvement, not guarantee employment.

## 🛠 Development Approach

LaunchPad AI will be built incrementally throughout the Frontend AI Engineering track.

Development principles:

- Small, meaningful iterations  
- Reusable components  
- Accessibility first  
- Responsive design  
- Maintainable code  
- Practical user experiences  
- AI‑assisted development with human review  

As stated in the project guidelines:  
“AI-generated code must be reviewed by the developer before being accepted.”

---

## 🎨 Product Principles

LaunchPad AI should be:

- **Useful** — practical, actionable guidance  
- **Clear** — simple explanations and next steps  
- **Trustworthy** — no fabricated candidate information  
- **Accessible** — usable across devices and abilities  
- **Encouraging** — progress-focused  
- **Action-oriented** — every analysis should lead to a next step
